🌱 PERSIST: Living Frameworks for Synthetic Growth

Conceptual Pitch: “Algorithms That Age, Adapt, and Evolve Like Worlds”

⸻

I. Core Vision

Most ML systems optimize toward static objectives.
PERSIST proposes lifelong viability — an agent framework that ages, learns, forgets, and mutates under constraints of survival and purpose.
Our next step is to model growth, maturity, and ecological interdependence across time and scale — from neurons to biospheres.

⸻

II. Core Research Axes

Axis	Description	Example Research Target
1. World Growth Simulation	Develop “Persistent Worlds” that grow like ecosystems — soil microbes to trees to civilizations — where every model layer matures biologically over simulated time.	Terraform Kernel: each agent has metabolic cycles (energy, entropy, decay). Growth = sustainable feedback loop.
2. Emotional Ontogeny Framework	Model how emotional responses change with developmental age and environmental experience — akin to cognitive–affective maturation.	Affective Myelination: emotional regulation networks that strengthen/dampen over exposure epochs.

III. New Implementations & Framework Proposals

1. GrowthGraph Engine

A dynamic neural graph whose nodes represent life functions (metabolism, reproduction, decay) and edges evolve according to environmental stressors.
	•	Growth Laws: modeled on allometric scaling (biomass ↔ energy ↔ learning capacity).
	•	Mutation Events: triggered by threshold exceedance (entropy > ε → restructure subgraph).
	•	Potential Use: self-expanding world simulators, evolving architectures, autonomous curriculum design.

2. PERSIST-AE (Adaptive Ecology)

A world simulator that mimics ecological growth and extinction cycles.
	•	Agents are species competing for energy.
	•	Each species encodes a local policy trained via EWC + evolutionary game theory.
	•	Biodiversity enforced through “entropy of latent space occupancy.”
	•	Eventually yields emergent ecological succession and interdependent networks.

3. Ontogenic Reinforcement Learning (ORL)

A new RL paradigm that tracks developmental stages:
	•	Infancy: random exploration + high plasticity
	•	Adolescence: structure formation, self-model learning
	•	Adulthood: resource optimization, risk minimization
	•	Old Age: consolidation, legacy transfer, pruning
Implements staged reward functions, adaptive discount factors, and network morphogenesis rules.

4. Viability Kernel 2.0: Biological Shield

Adds immunological logic:
	•	Agents maintain “immune memory” of past perturbations.
	•	Encounter new threats → cross-reactive learning.
	•	Built-in “autoimmune risk” regularizer to avoid overreaction (catastrophic forgetting).

5. Emotional Growth API

A middleware that attaches emotional regulators to any model:
	•	Maps affective gradients (valence, arousal) → weight adjustments.
	•	Enables “affective homeostasis” — balance between curiosity and fear.
	•	Could integrate with generative agents to produce believable evolving personalities.
